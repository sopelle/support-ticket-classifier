# Account & Billing

## FAQ

### How do I create an account?

Sign up at the platform with a work email, then create your organization and invite teammates from Settings > Users. The first user to sign up is automatically an Admin.

### What plans are available?

Starter, Growth, and Enterprise. Plans differ by number of frameworks included, integration limits, and whether SSO is available; each additional framework beyond what's included is billed separately.

### How do I update my payment method?

From Settings > Billing > Payment Method. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle; there's no way to retroactively apply a new payment method to an already-processed charge.

### Where can I find past invoices?

Settings > Billing > Invoices lists every past charge with a downloadable PDF receipt, useful for expense reports or your own audit trail.

### Is billing seat-based?

Yes, on Growth and Enterprise plans, billing scales with the number of active users (Admin and Contributor roles; Viewer seats, commonly used for auditors, are free). Starter plans have a flat seat cap.

### How do I add or remove seats?

Seats adjust automatically as you invite or remove users, no separate step needed. Your next invoice reflects the seat count as of the billing date.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

From Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period; you keep access until then and can export your data at any time before it ends.

### Who can manage billing on my team?

Only Admins can view or change billing details. You can have multiple Admins if more than one person needs billing access.

## Troubleshooting

### My invoice charged the wrong seat count

Seat count is calculated from active Admin and Contributor users on your billing date, not today. If someone was removed after that date, the change will show correctly on your next invoice rather than this one.

### Updating my payment method is failing

This is usually the card issuer declining the verification charge, common with corporate cards that block small automated charges. Ask your card issuer to allow charges from the platform, or try a different card.

### My downgrade isn't reflected on the next invoice

Plan downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle, not immediately, so an invoice generated shortly after a downgrade may still reflect the old plan. Check the invoice date against your renewal date.

### I'm missing a receipt or invoice for a past charge

All invoices should appear under Settings > Billing > Invoices going back to account creation. If one is missing, it may have been a one-time adjustment processed manually, contact support with the charge date and amount to get a copy.
