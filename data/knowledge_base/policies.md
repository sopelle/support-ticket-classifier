# Policies

## FAQ

### What policy templates are available?

The library includes templates for the policies most frameworks require: Information Security, Access Control, Incident Response, Data Retention, Acceptable Use, and about 15 others. Each template is pre-mapped to the controls it satisfies.

### Can I customize a policy template?

Yes. Templates are fully editable in the built-in editor: add company-specific details, remove sections that don't apply, or write a policy from scratch. Edits don't affect the control mapping unless you remove a section the mapping depends on.

### How does policy approval work?

A policy moves from Draft to Pending Approval when its author submits it, then to Published once a designated approver (usually a manager or the security lead) signs off. You can configure who's allowed to approve for each policy.

### How do employees acknowledge a policy?

Once published, employees are prompted to read and acknowledge the policy on their next login, or via an emailed reminder. Acknowledgment status per employee is visible on the policy's detail page.

### How often should policies be reviewed?

Most frameworks expect an annual review at minimum. The platform tracks each policy's last-reviewed date and flags it for re-approval when it's overdue.

### Can I export a policy as a PDF?

Yes, from the policy's detail page. The export includes the current published version and a signature/acknowledgment summary if you need it for an auditor.

### How are policies mapped to controls?

Each template comes pre-mapped; if you write a custom policy, you can map it manually to any control it addresses so it counts as evidence for that control.

### What happens to acknowledgments when I publish a new policy version?

Prior acknowledgments apply to the version they were given for. Publishing a new version resets acknowledgment status and re-prompts all employees to review and acknowledge the update.

## Troubleshooting

### An employee's acknowledgment isn't registering as complete

Check whether they acknowledged an older version, acknowledgments don't carry over across versions (see the FAQ above). Also confirm they're on an active roster; acknowledgments from deactivated accounts don't count toward completion.

### A policy is stuck in "Pending Approval"

The designated approver hasn't acted on it yet. Confirm the right person is actually configured as approver for that policy, if the approver was removed from the account, the policy has no one who can approve it until a new approver is assigned.

### The exported PDF is missing recent edits

PDF export always reflects the last *published* version, not unsaved drafts. Publish your edits first, then re-export.

### A policy reverted to an older version unexpectedly

This happens if two editors were working on the same policy simultaneously and one submitted a draft based on an outdated version. Check the version history on the policy's detail page to recover the newer draft's content.
