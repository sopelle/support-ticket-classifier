# Audits

## FAQ

### How do I choose an auditor?

You can bring your own licensed CPA firm (for SOC 2) or accredited certification body (for ISO 27001), or request an introduction from our partner network under Audits > Find an Auditor.

### How do I invite my auditor to the platform?

From Audits > Invite Auditor, enter their email. They get their own login scoped to auditor access; you don't need to share your admin credentials.

### What access does an auditor get?

Read-only access to the controls, evidence, and policies in scope for the specific audit they're assigned to. They can't see controls or evidence outside that framework's scope, and can't make changes.

### What is the audit readiness report?

A snapshot you can generate and share with your auditor before fieldwork starts, showing control status and evidence coverage so they know what's ready to review versus still in progress.

### How long does an audit take from fieldwork to report?

Fieldwork itself (the auditor's review) typically takes 1-3 weeks depending on scope and how quickly evidence requests are answered. Report issuance after fieldwork concludes usually takes another 1-2 weeks from the auditor.

### What happens during fieldwork?

Your auditor reviews evidence, may request samples beyond what's already uploaded, and interviews control owners for anything that needs clarification. Requests and responses happen directly in the platform so there's a record for both sides.

### How do I download the final report or certificate?

Once your auditor issues it, it appears under Audits > Reports. Only Admins can download the final report by default; you can grant other roles access from there.

### Do I need to redo the whole audit every year?

For SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 surveillance audits, no, most of the control set carries over and the auditor focuses on the period since the last report plus any changes to your environment. A full recertification audit for ISO 27001 happens every three years.

## Troubleshooting

### My auditor says they can't access requested evidence

Confirm the evidence is attached to a control that's actually in scope for the audit they're assigned to, evidence on out-of-scope controls isn't visible to them. Also check their auditor invite hasn't expired.

### The audit readiness report shows outdated data

The report is a point-in-time snapshot, it doesn't update automatically as controls change. Generate a new one from Audits > Readiness Report to reflect current status.

### The auditor invite link expired before they used it

Invite links expire after 7 days, same as teammate invites. Resend the invite from Audits > Invite Auditor to generate a fresh link.

### The final report or certificate isn't downloadable

This is usually a role issue, only Admins can download by default (see the FAQ above). If you are an Admin and it's still not downloadable, the auditor may not have marked the audit as "Issued" yet on their end.
